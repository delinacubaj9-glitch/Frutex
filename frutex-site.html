About Frutex

From Suhareka to international markets.

Founded in 1994 in Suhareka, Kosovo, Frutex began as a family business focused on producing and selling beverages. Over the years, it has grown into a diversified company with a portfolio that includes Golden Eagle, Relax, Natural 100%, and Art Food Industry.

Frutex introduced Golden Eagle in 2006, launched Relax in 2010, invested in fruit processing and plantations in 2018, and introduced Natural 100% in 2020. Today, the company has established a strong export presence, reaching more than 30 countries.
